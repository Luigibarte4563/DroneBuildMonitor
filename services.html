FAQ

Do I need a permit for the flight?+

No. Our pilots are licensed and handle the clearances. We only need your permission to fly over the site.

What if it rains on the flight date?+

We move the flight to the next clear day at no extra cost.

Do you fly outside the Philippines?+

No. We only serve sites in the Philippines.

How do I pay?+

GCash, Maya, credit or debit card, bank transfer (BDO or BPI), or cash on site. Prices exclude 12% VAT.
